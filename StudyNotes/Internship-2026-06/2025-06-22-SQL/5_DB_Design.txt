Database Name: AttendanceDB

Instructors
- InstructorID (PK, int)
- FirstName (varchar(20))
- LastName (varchar(20))

Students
- StudentID (PK, int)
- FirstName (varchar(20))
- LastName (varchar(20))
- StudentNumber (varchar(20))

Classes
- ClassID (PK, int)
- ClassName (varchar(20))
- InstructorID (FK, int) 1:N

ClassStudents (Junction) N:N
- ClassID (FK, int)
- StudentID (FK, int) 

Sessions
- SessionID (PK, int)
- ClassID (FK, int)
- SessionDate (date veya datetime2)

Attendances (Junction Table)
- SessionID (FK, int)
- StudentID (FK, int)
- IsPresent (bit)
